# Helping and Mentoring

Lots of guild members would happily help others: answering class questions, tanking a dungeon, showing a new player the ropes. Nobody knows who to ask, though. Helping and Mentoring lets members say **what they're willing to help with**, so anyone can find **who in the guild might help me with this?**

It builds on [Identity](identity.md) (your profile), the [Guild Roster](roster.md), and [Discovery](discovery.md).

## Saying what you'll help with

In **Settings → Asgard's Guild Fellowship → My profile → I can help with**, tick any of these:

| Topic |
| --- |
| New players |
| Class questions |
| Tanking |
| Healing |
| Quests |
| Dungeons |
| PvP |
| Professions |
| General mentoring |

- **It belongs to you, not your character.** Set it on any of your characters and it covers all of them.
- **It stays set until you change it.** This is different from [availability](discovery.md), which is about what you're doing *right now* and clears itself.
- **It's shared** with guildmates who use the add-on as part of your profile, so they can find you even while you're offline.
- **Only you can change it.** Guildmates' add-ons only accept changes that come from one of your own characters.

Untick everything to stop offering help.

## Finding help

| Where | How | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guild Roster** (`/gf roster`) | **Can help with** dropdown | Everyone who helps with that topic, online or offline (turn off **Show offline people** to see only those online). Kept until you reload. |
| **Discovery** (main panel) | **Can help with** dropdown | Helpers online right now. Saved with your other Discovery filters. Busy people stay hidden unless **Show Busy** is ticked. |

People who have picked topics show them everywhere they appear:

- **Guild Roster** headline: `Final · online as Shiro (Elwynn Forest) · Helps with: Tanking, Quests`
- **Discovery** row: `… · Helps with: Healing, General mentoring`
- **`/gf who <name>`**: a `Helps with: …` line

There's no "ask for help" button and nothing is posted for you. When you find someone, use the **Whisper** button or just say hello.
